Role of Indian Courts in Shaping Societal Norms and Beliefs

Explores how judicial decisions act as cultural catalysts, redefining ethics and altering public behaviour. The judiciary is framed as a shaper of social consciousness—not merely an interpreter of legal doctrine.

Indian Media and Digital Democracy

Analyses how digital media cultures redefine political authority, public discourse, and citizen psychology. It investigates how information ecosystems influence legitimacy, polarization, and democratic resilience.

Queer Linguistics and Cultural Meaning

Examines how language constructs identity, visibility, and exclusion. The work maps the evolution of queer vocabulary and explores how linguistic agency empowers or restricts marginalized communities.
