Sirr-e-Ulfat (Original Poetry)

A collection of self-composed ghazals, letters, verses, and intimate reflections, Sirr-e-Ulfat stands at the confluence of classical Urdu romance and contemporary emotional truth. It holds the ache of unspoken confessions, the warmth of beloved memories, and the serenity that follows acceptance. Many readers say the book feels as though someone is narrating their own unvoiced emotions.

Sirr-e-Nihaan (Curated & Transliterated Works)

This book gathers my Urdu writings—transliterated into Hindi without losing their cultural texture or poetic cadence. It is a tapestry of feelings veiled in language: couplets, fragments, ghazals, and whispers of affection. Every letter seeks to comfort, to soothe, and to remind the reader of the intimacy between poetry and the human heart. It is a gentle invitation to recognise yourself—your longing, your silence, your tenderness.

UnErased (Raw Thoughts and Emotional Reckonings)

UnErased is a journey into the rawest landscapes of the mind—written during moments of deep distress, heartbreak, and existential searching. These are not “poems,” but unfiltered thoughts, questions, and emotional bursts that escaped onto paper when the world felt unbearably heavy.
