Institutional Behaviour & Public Psychology

How do judicial and administrative decisions influence emotion, trust, and civic behaviour? Kaif aims to examine institutional actions not only as legal interventions but as psychological signals.

Ethics of Digital Power & Narrative Construction

As political legitimacy is increasingly built through digital narratives, Kaif plans to explore:

  • accountability in digital influence
  • disinformation and moral distortion
  • ethical media frameworks for democratic stability

Language as Social Justice Infrastructure

Building on queer linguistics, Kaif will investigate how language shapes identity rights, cultural belonging, and inclusion within constitutional democracies.
